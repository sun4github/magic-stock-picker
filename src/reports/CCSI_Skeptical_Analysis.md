### Basic Company Info Questions

**1. What products or services does the company actually sell?**
Consensus Cloud Solutions, Inc. (CCSI) provides cloud-based secure document delivery and data exchange services. Its core product suite revolves around digital cloud faxing (under flagship brands like **eFax Corporate®**, **ECFax®**, **MyFax®**, **SFax®**, and **SRFax®**), electronic signature tools (**jSign®**), and specialized healthcare interoperability and data extraction tools (**eFax Unite™**, **eFax Conductor**, and **eFax Clarity™**).

**2. Who are its primary customers?**
Its primary customers are organizations operating in heavily regulated industries that require secure, compliant document transmission (such as healthcare providers, government agencies, legal firms, financial institutions, and education), as well as small office/home office (SoHo) individual subscribers and small businesses.

**3. How does the company make money?**
CCSI generates revenue primarily through Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) monthly recurring subscription fees (representing approximately 67% of total subscription revenue in 2025), alongside usage-based fees charged when customers exceed their allotted monthly page or transmission quotas.

**4. Who are its two closest publicly traded competitors that compete most directly in the company’s largest revenue source, and what makes this company different from them? Explain why you chose those two.**
* **OneSpan Inc. (OSPN):** Competes directly in secure electronic document delivery, enterprise identity verification, and e-signatures. CCSI differs by being deeply rooted in digital fax protocols and FedRAMP/HITRUST-certified healthcare data workflows.
* **Backblaze, Inc. (BLZE):** Competes as a specialized public software provider of cloud data storage and transmission infrastructure. CCSI differs by offering domain-specific document intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP/AI) data extraction tailored to medical records rather than general cloud storage.
* *Why chosen:* Both OSPN and BLZE represent small-cap, publicly traded cloud/software vendors specializing in secure enterprise document delivery, data handling, and compliance workflows.

**5. Write one sentence I could use to explain this business to a friend with no investing experience.**
"Consensus Cloud Solutions helps companies send, receive, and automatically extract information from sensitive paperwork over the internet securely, eliminating the need for old-fashioned paper fax machines."

**6. What is one common misunderstanding or oversimplification that could lead an investor to analyze this company incorrectly?**
The most common oversimplification is assuming CCSI is merely a dying "online fax company" bound for rapid extinction. While legacy digital fax is an aging technology, it remains heavily mandated in healthcare and legal sectors due to regulatory rules. Investors often overlook that CCSI utilizes its immense eFax cash flow to fund high-margin software transitions, such as AI-driven medical document extraction (*eFax Clarity*).

**7. Using the most recent annual report, what are the main business segments of the company, which generates the most revenue, and which generates the most profit? If those are different segments, explain why.**
* **Corporate Solutions:** Tailored to enterprise, healthcare, and government accounts (approx. 65 thousand customers). Generated **$223 million** in revenue in 2025.
* **SoHo Fax Solutions:** Tailored to individual users and small businesses (approx. 638 thousand customers). 
* *Revenue & Profit:* The **Corporate Solutions** segment generates both the most total revenue (approx. 64% of consolidated revenue) and the highest operating profit, driven by larger contract sizes, lower relative customer churn, and higher average revenue per account (ARPA).

### Business Model Questions

**1. What is the business model of the company?**
CCSI operates a cloud-based SaaS model. Customers pay recurring monthly or annual subscriptions to access secure transmission infrastructure, compliance tools, APIs, and document extraction applications.

**2. Is revenue recurring, one-time, or repeat but non-contractual?**
Revenue is primarily **recurring subscription** (67% fixed monthly base), supplemented by **repeat usage-based charges** for high-volume document senders.

**3. Where does pricing power come from?**
Pricing power stems from **high switching costs and regulatory compliance lock-in**. Replacing an enterprise document pipeline requires deep re-integration with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems and re-certification under strict HIPAA, HITRUST, and FedRAMP High Impact security protocols.

**4. What would happen to revenue if their best-selling product was made obsolete by a competitor tomorrow?**
If digital cloud faxing (eFax Corporate/SoHo) became obsolete overnight, CCSI’s top line would **collapse by over 70% to 80%**, as its newer interoperability and AI products (Clarity, Conductor) still rely heavily on incoming fax documents as their primary raw data input.

**5. Based on disclosures in the most recent annual report, are any customers responsible for more than 10% of revenue? If this is not disclosed in the filing, say "not disclosed" rather than guessing.**
Based on the 2025 Form 10-K filing, no single customer accounts for more than 10% of revenue (its top 10 customers combined represent approximately $33 million, or 9% of total revenue).

**6. Based on the most recent annual report, what percentage of revenue comes from the top product or business segment? How has that percentage changed over the last three years?**
The **Corporate Solutions** segment represented approximately **64% of total revenue ($223 million)** in 2025. This segment's share of total revenue has increased significantly over the last three years (up from $170 million or ~48% of total revenue in 2021), reflecting an intentional strategic pivot toward enterprise clients while letting consumer SoHo accounts decline.

**8. Which segment generates the most profit, and is that different from the one generating the most revenue?**
The **Corporate Solutions** segment generates the most profit, which aligns with it generating the most revenue.
