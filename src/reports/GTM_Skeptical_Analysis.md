### **Basic Company Info Questions**

1. **What products or services does the company actually sell?**  
   ZoomInfo Technologies Inc. (GTM) sells a cloud-based go-to-market (GTM) intelligence and workflow software platform. Its core offerings include contact and company databases, buyer intent signals, sales engagement tools, automated lead routing, and talent acquisition tools sold under product lines like *ZoomInfo Copilot*, *ZoomInfo Sales*, *ZoomInfo Marketing*, *ZoomInfo Operations*, and *ZoomInfo Talent*.

2. **Who are its primary customers?**  
   Its primary customers are sales, marketing, operations, and recruiting teams across various industries, including software, manufacturing, telecommunications, financial services, and professional services. Customer size ranges from small-and-medium businesses (SMBs) to large global enterprise accounts.

3. **How does the company make money?**  
   ZoomInfo generates revenue primarily through subscription-based software contracts. Subscriptions are priced based on platform functionality, number of user licenses (seats), and the volume of records managed or exported, with a gradual transition toward usage/credit-based pricing models.

4. **Who are its two closest publicly traded competitors that compete most directly in the company’s largest revenue source, and what makes this company different from them? Explain why you chose those two.**  
   * **Dun & Bradstreet (DNB)** and **TechTarget (TTGT)** (alongside Microsoft’s LinkedIn Sales Navigator). DNB and TechTarget were chosen because they directly compete in B2B corporate contact intelligence, intent data, and sales enablement databases—ZoomInfo's primary revenue driver. ZoomInfo differentiates itself by integrating a proprietary machine-learning data engine with a contributory network and workflow automation (e.g., ZoomInfo Copilot) directly into customer CRMs, rather than relying solely on legacy static business directories (like DNB) or publisher-network intent lists (like TechTarget).

5. **Write one sentence I could use to explain this business to a friend with no investing experience.**  
   *ZoomInfo sells a digital phonebook and research software that helps business sales teams quickly find verified email addresses, phone numbers, and buying signals for prospective business clients.*

6. **What is one common misunderstanding or oversimplification that could lead an investor to analyze this company incorrectly?**  
   Investors often oversimplify ZoomInfo as just a static "contact list broker" vulnerable to being immediately rendered obsolete by modern AI search tools. This oversimplification ignores ZoomInfo’s deeply embedded workflow integrations into customer CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot) and automated routing tools; conversely, it can also lead bulls to overlook how severely downmarket seat reductions and low-cost AI scraping tools erode ZoomInfo's pricing power among SMB clients.

7. **Using the most recent annual report, what are the main business segments of the company, which generates the most revenue, and which generates the most profit? If those are different segments, explain why.**  
   ZoomInfo operates and reports as a **single operating segment**: the go-to-market intelligence platform. All revenue ($1.24 billion in FY2025) and operating income ($225.7 million in FY2025) originate from this single integrated platform. Core sales intelligence solutions (*ZoomInfo Sales / Copilot*) drive the vast majority of subscription revenue.
